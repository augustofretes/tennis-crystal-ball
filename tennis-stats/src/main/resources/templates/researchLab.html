The scoring laboratory

Give Player 1 a chance of winning each point on serve and return. Compare twelve combinations of match length, advantage scoring, and deciding-set tiebreaks.

0.65 means 65%. Point probabilities stay fixed throughout the match. No-ad uses a single deciding point at deuce; super tiebreaks are first to ten, win by two, at 6–6 in the deciding set. These are model scenarios, not measured effects on real players.
