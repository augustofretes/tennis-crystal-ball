Endpoints and filters
GET /api/v1/players
q (2–100 characters), country (three letters), active (true/false). Retrieve a profile with /players/{id}.
GET /api/v1/matches
playerId, eventId, fromSeason, toSeason, surface (H/C/G/P), level, outcome (all/completed). Detail at /matches/{id} includes available aggregate serve counts.
GET /api/v1/events
Season, surface and level filters. Detail at /events/{id}; use its event ID to find matches or open the historical draw.
GET /api/v1/rankings
date selects the latest available ranking snapshot on or before an ISO date. Without a date, use today.
GET /api/v1/players/{id}/ratings
fromDate and toDate select recorded overall and surface Elo snapshots.
All lists
limit (1–200; default 50), offset (0–100000), format (json/csv). CSV contains the requested page. Unknown, duplicate and invalid parameters return HTTP 400.
Experiments
GET /api/v1/lab/rules, POST /api/v1/predictions/match, POST /api/v1/simulations. Try the Research Lab for working examples.

			
Coverage, sources and interpretation
Results cover men's main-tour singles. Player and ranking registries can include players without main-tour matches. Qualifying, Challenger and point-by-point expansion are tracked separately. A recorded match date may be a tournament date when the exact day is unavailable. Coverage dates describe the latest recorded observations; they do not guarantee a complete season or a historical snapshot.
Missing statistics are unknown, not zero. w_ and l_ identify winner and loser serve counts. ATP ranking dates and Elo dates can differ. Rankings requested for a past date use today's corrected database.
Historical data is attributed to Jeff Sackmann / Tennis Abstract. Current result ingestion reconciles ESPN and Tennis Abstract. See the repository's source and attribution notes. Refresh and correction work may change records between requests, so offset pagination does not provide a frozen dataset. Coverage is cached for up to five minutes.
